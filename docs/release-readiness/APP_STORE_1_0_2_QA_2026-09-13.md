# BrassTune 1.0.2 (9) release QA — September 13, 2026
Project attribution: Kelvin Guo, Arya Salem.

## Scope and release boundary

The owner requested a broad Luna/Terra test sweep and release through Safari. No finite test suite proves an app bug-free. This native update covers sign-in/guest-practice decisions, metronome PCM playback and duplex tuner audio, drone transitions, and focused metronome controls/visual cues. The web/backend suites are regression checks; this task does not deploy those services.

## Findings and test limitations

- Compact testing initially encountered a killed XCTest process and an outer-command timeout. These were not treated as successful runs or as proven product layout defects. All three main-simulator Dynamic Type/RTL/orientation tests passed; the final compact rerun also passed all three checks.
- New section UI tests initially failed on offscreen SwiftUI elements. The test helper was corrected to scroll the actual container and instantiate lazy content before assertions; final three-test rerun passed.
- Existing AirPlay tests expect uninterrupted playback across route changes, while the implemented route policy stops output and asks for restart. Those stale tests do not establish accessory-route reliability.
- A pre-existing web-only Progress inconsistency was found: date filtering affects statistics/heatmap but recommendations and practice-plan endpoints use all-time data. The native app does not call those endpoints. It is outside this App Store binary update and remains open.
- Provider consent/cancellation probes do not prove completed sign-in for every real Apple/Google account. Physical acoustic captures are uncalibrated and do not certify all accessories, volumes or instruments.

## Post-release checks

1. Install 1.0.2 from the App Store and confirm its version/build in Settings. Preserve an existing guest practice history before updating; confirm it survives the update.
2. From Settings and Classes, try Apple and Google sign-in. With guest history, confirm only merge, separate and cancel are offered; test cancel/retry and confirm the chosen history remains correct.
3. Run the metronome at 60, 120 and 180 BPM with quarter notes, triplets and sixteenths; enable the tuner, change tempo repeatedly, then stop/restart. Listen for clear, evenly timed beats at moderate volume.
4. While playing a drone, change notes, intervals and volume. Confirm uninterrupted tone, no abrupt pops, and immediate Stop behavior.
5. Open metronome Settings and the tuner’s drone/interval entry. Confirm controls stay visible on small screens and larger text; play below/above pitch and check the directional cue.
6. Save a tuner take, finish a warm-up/scale, import a score and review Progress. Verify saved data after relaunch.
7. For Classes, check student, owner and assistant views using authorized test memberships; confirm sharing consent and role boundaries.
8. On wired/Bluetooth/AirPlay routes, verify route changes and explicit restart behavior separately. Check interruption/background recovery and microphone-denied recovery.

Rollback: before approval, remove the version from review if a blocker appears. After public release, Apple does not provide an instant binary downgrade; submit a corrected build and use available release controls to limit further rollout. Do not erase users’ practice data as a workaround.
